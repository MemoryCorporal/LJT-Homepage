## About Me

I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with active interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM), as well as LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Academic Background
- **Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present)**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Prof. Junxian He
- **B.Eng. (2020-2024)**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduated in June 2024, awarded the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship

## Research Experience
- **Research Intern (February 2025 - Present)**, MINIMAX (AI technology company)
- **Research Intern (June 2024 - September 2024)**, Tencent WXG, advised by Zifei Shan
- **Research Intern (June 2023 - December 2023)**, Shanghai AI Lab, advised by Prof. Yu Cheng
